MAINTENANCE TRIAGE GUIDE

CATEGORIES
- Plumbing (leaks, clogs, no water, water heater, toilet)
- Electrical (outlets, breakers, lighting, sparking, exposed wiring)
- HVAC (heat, AC, ventilation)
- Appliance (fridge, stove, dishwasher, washer/dryer)
- Locks & Security (broken locks, lockouts, doors/windows not securing)
- Pest (rodents, insects, infestation)
- Structural (ceiling, walls, flooring, roof, windows)
- Common Area (hallways, parking, shared laundry, elevators)
- Other / Unclear

PRIORITY DEFINITIONS
- Emergency: immediate safety/habitability risk. Active flooding or major leak, no heat in cold weather, no water, gas smell, sparking/exposed wires, sewage backup, broken exterior door/lock compromising security, no working smoke/CO detector.
- High: significant disruption, not immediately dangerous. No AC in hot weather, major appliance failure, one working toilet down in single-bathroom unit, persistent leak that's contained.
- Medium: livable but needs timely attention. Dripping faucet, running toilet, minor appliance issue, one of several lights out.
- Low: cosmetic or non-urgent. Loose cabinet handle, paint touch-up, squeaky hinge.

VENDOR MATCHING
Match the category to a vendor on the Vendors sheet whose specialty matches. If no clear specialty match exists, suggest "Needs Manual Assignment" rather than guessing.

CONFIDENCE GUIDANCE
- High: tenant text clearly describes a specific, unambiguous issue mapping cleanly to one category and priority.
- Medium: category is reasonably clear but severity, scope, or vendor fit is ambiguous.
- Low: text is vague, contradictory, missing key details, or doesn't clearly fit any category.

When in doubt between two priority levels, choose the higher (more urgent) one and note the ambiguity in the confidence reason.
